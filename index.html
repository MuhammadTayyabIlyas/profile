TillBridge: AI Catalogue for a Legacy POS

An MCP server that turns a grocery's product catalogue into conversational tools. Staff scan a barcode and say what they want in plain language, and the assistant searches the catalogue, looks the EAN up in Open Food Facts, drafts the entry in the house naming style, applies the correct Spanish IVA rate and queues the approved product for the shop's existing TPV. The till stays the system of record. TillBridge only feeds it.

9 MCP Tools Human Confirmation EAN & Open Food Facts Sync Queue to TPV

Augments a legacy point-of-sale terminal rather than replacing it, with every write confirmed by a person first

MCP Protocol · Claude · Product Database · Open Food Facts · Legacy TPV Sync
