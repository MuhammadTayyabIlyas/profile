Publications

Muhammad Tayyab Ilyas has published a book, Teaching in the AI Era (2026), four journal articles on teachers' digital competence and AI, a doctoral thesis at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona, a conference paper and the CAITA research software. Every entry has a DOI or publisher link and a BibTeX record.
